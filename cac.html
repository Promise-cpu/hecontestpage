What You Need to Provide

📄 CAC Registration

  • Proposed business/company names (2-3 options)
  • Business activities description
  • Valid ID (NIN, Passport, Driver's License)
  • Passport photograph
  • Business address (physical or virtual)
  • Shareholders/Directors details (for LTD)

⚖️ Trademark & SCUML

  • Trademark: Logo or wordmark, class of goods/services, applicant details
  • SCUML: Certificate of Incorporation, Tax ID, business address, director details
  • Nature of designated non-financial business (real estate, legal, accounting, etc.)
  • Valid means of identification for directors

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does CAC registration take?

Business Name: 1-3 working days. Limited Liability Company: 5-7 working days, subject to CAC approval.

What is a trademark and why register it?

A trademark protects your brand name, logo, or slogan. Registration grants exclusive rights and legal protection against infringement in Nigeria.

Who needs an EFCC SCUML certificate?

Designated Non-Financial Businesses and Professions (DNFBPs) such as real estate agents, lawyers, accountants, dealers in luxury goods, etc.

Can I register a trademark and SCUML simultaneously?

Yes, we can handle both processes concurrently. Our team coordinates with the Trademark Registry and EFCC to ensure full compliance.
